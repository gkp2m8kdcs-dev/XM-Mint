Features

Everything serious investors need.

A complete personal-finance cockpit, hand-crafted in Swift with custom-drawn interactive charts.

📊

Net Worth Dashboard

Live hero card with sparkline, KPI tiles for income, expenses, savings rate and portfolio value, plus an interactive cash-flow chart across 7D / 30D / 90D / YTD.

💼

Investments & Portfolios

Stocks, ETFs, bonds, mutual funds, crypto and real estate — with interactive price charts, position detail and one-tap price updates you control.

🏦

Multi-Account Tracking

Cash, checking, savings, credit and investment accounts unified in one balance view across your entire financial life.

🎯

Budgets & Goals

Monthly category budgets with progress rings that warn before you breach, plus savings goals with contribution tracking and deadline countdowns.

🧾

Receipt Capture

Attach camera or photo-library receipts to any transaction. View them in a dedicated gallery — encrypted on-device.

📈

5-Mode Reports

Monthly trend, category breakdown, cash flow, income sources and top merchants — visualized with custom bar, donut and stacked charts.

🔒

On-Device Storage

No accounts, no servers, no login. Your data lives only on your iPhone in local secure storage. You are the only one with access.

⚡

Built Native in Swift

100% UIKit, hand-drawn charts with CAShapeLayer & UIBezierPath. No SwiftUI, no third-party charting libraries, no bloat.

App Tracking Transparency

Tracking Declaration

Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) requires apps to disclose tracking activity. Here is ours, in plain English.

🛡️

XM Mint does NOT track you.

Period. We do not collect, share, or sell any data that identifies you or your device. XM Mint does not display advertising, and we do not operate any analytics or marketing SDK that follows you across other companies' apps or websites.

  • No IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers) access
  • No third-party analytics SDKs (Firebase, Amplitude, etc.)
  • No advertising networks or attribution SDKs
  • No cross-app or cross-site behavioral tracking
  • No personal data leaves your device — ever
  • The ATT permission prompt is therefore not required
