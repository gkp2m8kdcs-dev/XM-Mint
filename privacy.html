Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 17, 2026  •  App: XM Mint
🛡 App Tracking Transparency Disclosure

XM Mint does not track you. We do not collect, share, or sell any data that identifies you or your device. We do not display advertising. No third-party analytics, attribution, or advertising SDKs are integrated. Because we do not track, Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission prompt is not required and is never triggered.

1. Introduction

Welcome to XM Mint ("we", "us", "our", or the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our iOS application. We have written this policy in plain English because your privacy matters and you should not need a law degree to understand it.

The short version: we do not collect anything from you. XM Mint is a 100% on-device application — every transaction, receipt, account, budget, goal and investment you enter lives only on your iPhone. We have no servers, no accounts, no login, and no analytics.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

To be unambiguous, we do not collect, transmit, store, share, sell, rent, or trade any of the following:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number, address)
  • Device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV, advertising identifiers, MAC address, IMEI)
  • Financial data you enter into the App (transactions, balances, holdings, budgets, goals, receipts)
  • Location data (precise or coarse)
  • Photos or images beyond those you choose to attach to transactions (stored only locally)
  • Contact lists, calendar entries, or any data from your other apps
  • Usage analytics, telemetry, crash logs, or behavioral data
  • Biometric data (the App does not access Face ID, Touch ID, or any biometric sensor)
  • Health, fitness, or sensitive personal information

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

XM Mint stores all data locally on your device using iOS secure application storage. This includes:

  • Transactions, accounts, categories, budgets, goals, investments you create
  • Receipt images you attach (stored in the app's private document directory)
  • App preferences (theme, currency, sample-data state)

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export or share it yourself. If you delete the App, this data is permanently deleted from the device.

4. Tracking Disclosure (Apple App Tracking Transparency)

Does XM Mint track you? No.

Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework defines "tracking" as linking user or device data with third-party data for advertising purposes, or sharing such data with a data broker. XM Mint does none of this. Specifically:

  • We do not access the IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers).
  • We do not display advertising of any kind.
  • We do not share data with advertising networks, attribution platforms, or data brokers.
  • We do not integrate any third-party analytics SDKs (Firebase, Amplitude, Mixpanel, Adjust, AppsFlyer, Branch, Singular, etc.).
  • We do not engage in cross-app or cross-site behavioral tracking.

Because no tracking activity exists, the ATT permission prompt is not required and is never displayed by XM Mint. Our NSUserTrackingUsageDescription permission is not implemented because we have no tracking use case for it.

5. Permissions Requested by the App

XM Mint requests only the iOS permissions it needs to function. None of these permissions are used for tracking:

Camera (NSCameraUsageDescription)

Used solely so you can capture a receipt photo to attach to a transaction. Images are saved only to the app's local document directory. We do not transmit them anywhere.

Photo Library (NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription)

Used solely so you can pick an existing receipt image to attach to a transaction. We do not upload or share your photos.

No other permissions (Location, Microphone, Contacts, Calendar, Health, Motion, Bluetooth, Face ID, etc.) are requested.

6. Children's Privacy

XM Mint is intended for general audiences and is not directed to children under 13 (or under the age defined by local law). We do not knowingly collect any information from children. Because we collect nothing from anyone, this is structurally impossible by design.

7. International Data Transfers

XM Mint does not transfer any personal data across borders, because there is no data to transfer. If you are in the EEA, UK, California, or any other jurisdiction with strong privacy laws, this Policy confirms that no data leaves your device for our processing.

8. Your Rights

Since we do not collect data, there is nothing to access, correct, export, or delete from us. However, you have full control over the data XM Mint stores locally:

  • Access / Export: All data is visible inside the App and can be reviewed by you at any time.
  • Edit / Delete: You may edit or delete any record inside the App.
  • Erasure: Deleting the App removes all local data permanently. You may also reset the app's data from Settings within the App.

9. Third-Party Links & Services

XM Mint does not link to or embed third-party services, websites, or SDKs. If we ever add an external link, this Policy will be updated and the link will be made clear inside the App.

10. Security

All data is protected by iOS sandboxing. XM Mint does not run any background network connections, hosts no servers, and exposes no attack surface for data exfiltration. Because there is no network sync, there is no remote breach vector for your data.

Recommended best practices on your side: keep your iOS device updated, enable a device passcode / Face ID, and do not jailbreak your device.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the App or for legal reasons. The "Effective Date" at the top will always show when the Policy was last revised. Material changes, if any, will be announced inside the App.
