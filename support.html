Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions.

Where is my financial data stored?

Everything you enter into XM Mint — transactions, accounts, budgets, goals, investments and receipts — is stored locally on your iPhone in secure application storage. There is no cloud sync, no server, and no account. If you delete the App, your data is permanently erased from the device. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Does XM Mint track me or collect any data?

No. XM Mint does not track you, display advertising, or integrate any analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not access the IDFA (Apple's Identifier for Advertisers) and we do not transmit any data off your device. The App Tracking Transparency permission prompt is therefore not required and is never shown. Read the full Tracking Declaration.

Do I need to create an account?

No. XM Mint is account-free by design. There is no sign-up, login, email verification or password to remember. Just install the App and start tracking immediately.

Can I export my data?

Yes. All your records are accessible inside the App at any time. You may also back up your device using iTunes or iCloud (standard iOS backups), which will include XM Mint's local data.

How do I attach a receipt to a transaction?

Open any transaction, tap the receipt icon, then either take a new photo with the camera or pick an existing image from your photo library. Receipts are stored only on your device, attached to that transaction.

Why are the investment prices sometimes slightly off?

XM Mint is not a live-quote feed. Prices are entered or updated by you with a single tap from a price you obtained elsewhere (your broker, a news source, etc.). This keeps the App on-device, private, and never connects to brokerage APIs. We do not provide investment advice.

Can I use XM Mint on iPad or Mac?

XM Mint is currently designed for iPhone (iOS 16+). It runs on iPad in compatibility mode but is not optimized for iPad layouts. A native iPad / Mac version may be considered in the future.

How do I delete all my data?

Open the App, go to Settings → Reset / Clear Data, and confirm. Or simply delete the App from your device — all local data is removed with it.

Does XM Mint provide investment advice?

No. XM Mint is a tracking and visualization tool. It does not provide financial, investment, tax or legal advice. Any numbers shown are a reflection of the inputs you entered. Always consult a licensed professional for advice tailored to your situation.
